# Core Research Concept

The project focuses on myocardial energetics derived from non-invasive PV-loop analysis.

Important energetic measures include:
- Stroke Work (SW)
- Pressure–Volume Area (PVA)
- Myocardial Efficiency

Where 4D-flow data are actually analysed, relevant flow energetic measures may include:
- LV blood-flow kinetic energy;
- viscous energy loss;
- other validated 4D-flow-derived energetic variables available in the completed dataset.

The dissertation must maintain the conceptual distinction between:
1. **Myocardial mechanical energetics**
2. **Blood-flow/haemodynamic energetics**

These measures must not be described as interchangeable.
