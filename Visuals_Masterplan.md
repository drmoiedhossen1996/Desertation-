## How to read this plan
Each entry gives: **type**, **original vs reproduced**, **placement (after which paragraph)**, **content**, **exact data**, **caption**, **footnote**, **citation**, **copyright/licensing note**, and my **recommendation** (Include / Optional / Omit).

### Guiding principles
1. **Do not over-illustrate.** A narrative review of this length is well served by ~4 tables and ~4 figures. More than that dilutes the argument.
2. **Prefer original schematics and data-derived graphs over reproduced journal figures.** Summary statistics (means, medians, correlations) are *facts* and are not copyrightable — a graph you build from published numbers is your own original work and only needs a "Data from ref X" attribution. Reproducing a journal's actual figure/image requires the publisher's licence or permission.
3. **Every visual must be discussed in the text** at or near its placement, and must not introduce any datum not supported by a cited source.
